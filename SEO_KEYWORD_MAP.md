## Internal Linking Rules

- Link from `/solutions` to every solution page.
- Link from each solution page to three closely related solution pages.
- Link healthcare content to candidate pre-screening, AI interviews, and resume screening.
- Link staffing agency content to high-volume hiring, candidate screening, and recruiting automation.
- Link high-volume content to candidate screening, pre-screening, and resume screening.
- Link AI interview content to automated interviews and pre-screening where context makes sense.

## Avoid Cannibalization

- Use `/solutions/ai-recruiting-software` for broad platform searches.
- Use `/solutions/ai-ats` only for applicant tracking and pipeline searches.
- Use `/solutions/candidate-screening-software` for broad screening and shortlist searches.
- Use `/solutions/ai-resume-screening` only for resume/CV review searches.
- Use `/solutions/candidate-pre-screening-software` for practical early qualification searches.
- Use `/solutions/ai-interview-platform` for deeper live AI interview searches.
- Use regional pages for market coordination language, not copied platform copy.
