# Hiriq SEO Content Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Content Strategy

Hiriq should build topical authority around early-funnel recruiting automation: resume screening, candidate pre-screening, live AI interviews, recruiter review, staffing agencies, healthcare staffing, and high-volume hiring.

The best content angle is not “AI replaces recruiters.” It is “AI structures evidence so recruiters can move faster with human oversight.”

## Link Building Targets

- AI recruiting directories where Hiriq can be listed honestly.
- Startup directories and founder interview profiles.
- HR tech comparison sites that allow vendor submissions.
- Healthcare staffing technology roundups where the product fit is accurate.
- Recruitment agency operations blogs or partner content.
- Founder/company profiles that can link to the BoardroomPK interview and Hiriq website.

## Content Rules

- Use real screenshots, real team photos, and verified founder/customer proof.
- Do not use fake testimonials or invented ROI statistics.
- Mention human oversight and recruiter review in AI hiring pages.
- Avoid publishing doorway pages with swapped country names.
- Add sources to educational articles when discussing laws, studies, or market statistics.
